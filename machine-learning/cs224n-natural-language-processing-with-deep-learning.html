Introduction and Word Vectors

  • word vectors = word embedding: measure similarity by dot product

Word2vec

  • Idea
    • Interate center and outside words in a corpus
    • Calculate probability of center word given outside word (or vice versa)
    • Keep adjusting vectors to maximize the probability
  • Objective function
    • For each position \( t = {1 \dots T} \), predict context window of fixed size \( m \) given center word \( w_{t} \)
    • Data likelyhood: \[ \text{Likelihood} = L(\theta) = \prod_{t=1}^{T} \prod_{\substack{-m \leq j \leq m \\ j \neq 0}} P(w_{t+j} \mid w_t; \theta) \]
    • Objective function is average negative log likelihood \[J(\theta) = -\frac{1}{T}\log L(\theta) = -\frac{1}{T}\sum_{t=1}^{T} \sum_{\substack{-m \leq j \leq m \\ j \neq 0}} \log P(w_{t+j} \mid w_t; \theta) \]
    • To calculate \( P(w_{t+j} \mid w_t; \theta) \), use two vectors per \( w \)
      • \( v_{w} \) when \( w \) is center word
      • \( u_{w} \) when \( w \) is context word
  • Prediction function
    • For center word \( c \) and outside word \( o \) (This is an example of softmax function) \[ P(o|c) = \frac{\exp(u_o^T v_c)}{\sum_{w \in V} \exp(u_w^T v_c)} \]
  • Model training
    • \( \theta \) represents all model parameters
    • With \( d \) dimensional vectors and \( v \) many words \[ \theta = \begin{bmatrix} v_{aardvark} \\ v_a \\ \vdots \\ v_{zebra} \\ u_{aardvark} \\ u_a \\ \vdots \\ u_{zebra} \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^{2dV} \]

Word Vectors and Language Models

  • Two model variants
    • Skip-grams: predict context words given center word
    • Continuous bag of words: predict cent words given context word
  • Loss function variants
    • Native softmax
    • Hierarchical softmas
    • Negative sampling
